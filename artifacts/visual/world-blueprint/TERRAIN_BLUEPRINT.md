# TERRAIN_BLUEPRINT.md — intentional landforms (each with location + purpose)
1. Main silhouette: oval 480x340, cliff-bound W/N/E, soft S. No noise coastline;
   hand-placed headlands at (-210,60), (150,-130), (210,10) and coves S/E/W.
2. N highlands: rock mass 220x70 cresting 12-14, ridge line E-W, peak near (0,-115).
   Purpose: backdrop mass, waterfall source, windmill elevation.
3. Ridge-to-town slope: forested fall from 8m to plaza 3.5m over ~100m. No cliffs
   on the S face (settlement must climb readably); cliffs on N/E faces.
4. Settlement plateau: flattened pad ~140x120 at 3-4m under civic+residential.
   Edges feather into forest/grass, never a visible step.
5. Farm bench: E bench 110x70 at 2.5-4m, slight E tilt for drainage read.
6. Windmill knoll: local bump to 9-10 at (60,-125), connected to ridge by a col.
7. Waterfall system: SOURCE ridge notch (-30,-70) → CHANNEL (carved gully S) →
   DROP (cliff face at (-28,-45), ~5m) → POOL → STREAM to civic lake. Same
   pattern at W islet (twin drops) and S falls (single drop).
8. Harbor bay: drowned cove 90x70, depth to -5, sandy rim shallows, rock jaws at
   both points. Lighthouse rock: separate steep islet (11.5 peak over 8m radius).
9. Beaches: S cove (130m), E cove (60m), W cove (90m) — sand wedges overlapping
   grass, wet band at waterline, never floating discs.
10. Reef shelf: S offshore bench -1..-2.5 with coral heads +0.5-1 above shelf.
11. Outer islets: W (twin-fall stacks), SW (low hut platform), N skerry (bare rock).
12. Underwater: shelf -6 apron around all land, cliffs continue 5m below waterline.
Transitions: grass→sand (2m), sand→wet rock (1m), rock faces only where slope
demands. Authored in Blender (director instruction); noise only for detail.

# VEGETATION_BLUEPRINT.md — designed masses, not scatter
- Shore palm band: beaches/coves waterline +5..15m inland, lean seaward, clusters 3-7.
- Settlement fringe: broadleaf masses W/S of civic, framing (not blocking) plaza
  sightlines; flower/fruit accents near houses.
- Farm windbreaks: tree lines N and E of plots; NO trees inside plots.
- Ridge forest: dense broadleaf + bamboo gullies on N slopes below cliffs.
- Cliff tops: grass tufts + stunted shrubs only; no trees on rock faces.
- Waterfall mist zone: ferns/flowers within 15m of plunge pools.
- Reef: blue/purple coral heads + seaweed on shelf; nothing emergent.
- Islets: palms (SW hut, lighthouse grass), bare N skerry.
- Species/scale per existing assets (palm, broadleaf, bush, bamboo, grass,
  flowers, seaweed, corals); crowns need redesign pass (flat-rectangle + pole
  vetoed by director). Controlled variant sets (3 crowns/species), no per-tree RNG.

## One world, three views (director-confirmed)
All three depict the SAME island. ref3 = finished place; ref1/ref2 = its
production breakdown (layout, orthos, assets, materials, LODs, Blender notes).
Hierarchy: Hero → composition → assets → implementation.

## ref3 spatial facts (top-down reading, N up, meters @ 800x600 world)
- Main island ~480 E-W x ~340 N-S, cliff-ringed W/N/E, opening to beaches S/SE.
- N third = highlands: rock massif, peak, ridge running E-W, windmill on a
  knoll NE-of-center-high (~+60,-125 map), forests clothing the slopes.
- Waterfalls: N falls drop S off the ridge into streams feeding a central civic
  lake; W twin falls off the W islet; small S falls near SW beach.
- Civic core at center: circular plaza + fountain monument on a small lake,
  town hall N of plaza, residential ring W+S, market row E.
- Farm belt NE/E: 4+ tilled plots in rows + farmhouses + greenhouse at E edge.
- Harbor SE: bay cove, L-dock + harbor house, moored sail/rowboats; lighthouse
  on a separate rock islet E of the bay, joined by a footbridge.
- Outer islets: W falls islet, SW fishing islet (hut + pier), N skerry, E rocks.
- Beaches: S cove (largest), E cove, W cove. Reef gardens offshore S (coral +
  seaweed on a shallow shelf). Water: turquoise shallow → deep blue.
- Vegetation: palms on beaches/shore, broadleaf inland masses, bamboo near
  water, flowers/accents near settlement. Density highest at settlement fringe.

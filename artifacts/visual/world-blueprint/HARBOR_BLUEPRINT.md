# HARBOR_BLUEPRINT.md — geography first, props second
1. Bay: drowned cove at zone 8, jaws of rock W+E, sandy shallows rim, -5 center.
2. Shoreline: working waterfront (not beach): stone edging, mooring posts.
3. Docks: L-dock from W jaw + harbor house at root; spans clear of boat berths.
4. Berths: sailboat moored E, rowboat near dock steps, fishing boat mid-bay —
   all inside the bay, all inside hero framing (prior boats drifted out of frame).
5. Lighthouse: separate rock (zone 7), footbridge from E jaw; lamp emissive.
6. Upland link: path from dock root N into town (market row extends to harbor).
7. Working props: crates, barrels, rope coils, net racks, lantern posts —
   clustered at dock root, never scattered on sand.
8. Water: moored bob (presentation), foam at jaws + dock piles. Bay must read as
   ONE place: rock + dock + boats + lighthouse + path, composed together.

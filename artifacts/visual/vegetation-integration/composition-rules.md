# Composition rules (integration findings — candidates for rollout)
1. Masses: clusters of 3–9 with 30–60% overlap read as groves; isolated singles
   read as specimens (use sparingly, landmarks only).
2. Sightlines: keep 2 open cones per grove; palms as vertical accents, never walls.
3. Terrain fit: raycast grounding mandatory; slope>0.55 → tufts only (verified:
   ridge tufts sit on rock, not floating).
4. Density knob works: low=skeleton (structure visible), med=balanced,
   ref=lush. Correct choice is per-zone (settle med, wild ref, civic low).
5. Color: adjacent families must differ (palm blue-green vs broadleaf deep
   green reads well); flower doses ≤3 per 20m or they become noise.
6. Shadows: canopy shadows ground the composition; palm-frond shadows are a
   feature (keep frond geometry shadow-casting).

# Vegetation reference analysis (from ref3 hero, director-confirmed)
Date: 2026-09-18. Method: close reading of ref3-i2znmu.png against current renders.

## A. Families (observed, not assumed)
1. Tall palms — beach/shore bands, lean seaward, 4–6m, sparse crowns (6–9 fronds
   visible individually), trunks thin (Ø0.25) with slight curve. HIGHEST shore read.
2. Broad-canopy tropicals — inland masses, 5–8m, crowns WIDER than tall, layered
   2–3 foliage tiers, visible branch structure at edges, deep green with sunlit tops.
3. Medium round trees — 3–5m, single dense ovoid crown, settlement + lanes.
4. Small trees/saplings — 2–3m, thin crowns, clearings and young stands.
5. Shrubs — 0.8–1.5m blobs, foundation planting, path edges, beach backs.
6. Grass tufts — ground texture scale, NOT individual blades; clumps read at 5m+.
7. Flowering accents — pink/white/yellow clusters at houses, plaza, falls mist.
8. Bamboo/cane — wet gullies, vertical culm clusters with narrow leaves.
9. Coastal scrub — low wind-pruned forms on sand/rock, sparse.
10. Cliff clingers — grass/shrub pockets on ledges only, never trees on faces.
11. Aquatics — seaweed ribbons + blue/purple coral heads on reef shelf.
12. Crops — wheat rows (gold when mature), distinct from wild grass by ROWS.

## B. Silhouette language (per family: trunk/crown/negative space)
- Palm: trunk 8–10× crown-width thin, curve ≤10°, crown = starburst of
  SEPARATE arching fronds with gaps (never a solid ball). Asymmetry required.
- Broadleaf: trunk short (1/3 height), crown = 3 overlapping displaced blobs,
  widest at 60% height, ragged lower edge (branches peek), top dome sunlit.
- Round tree: single ellipsoid, slightly flattened, small visible trunk.
- Shrub: 2–3 merged spheres, wider than tall, flat-ish top.
- Current failure: fronds read as flat rectangles nailed around a pole (planar
  quads, no arch, no gaps pattern); crowns read as single boxes/spheres.

## C. Scale hierarchy (meters)
Canopy 6–8 → palm 4–6 → medium 3–5 → small 2–3 → shrub 0.8–1.5 → grass 0.3–0.5
→ flowers 0.2–0.4. Person (1.7) must read against houses (4.5) against canopy (7).

## D. Color hierarchy (locked families, no global saturation push)
- Deep foliage #2E7D3A (interior/shadow masses) → mid #3F9E4D → sunlit #5FBF5A
  → yellow-green accents #7FBF3F (new growth, flowers beds).
- Palm fronds blue-green #3A9E6E (distinct from broadleaf).
- Trunks grey-brown #6B4A35 (NOT orange timber).
- Flowers: pink #FF6EC7 / white #F4F1E8 / yellow #FFD94D, small doses.
- Crops: green → straw #D8A93F → harvest gold #FFB02E (emissive .15 at mature).
- Seaweed #2E7D5A, coral blue #3AA7C9 / purple #9B5DE5.

# Activity study (ref3 close-read per zone)

## Observed patterns (only what the reference shows)
- CIVIC: striped stalls + counter goods; benches facing fountain; banners on poles;
  lantern posts flanking paths; crate/barrel stacks at stall flanks; flower ring
  at plaza; fountain as gathering center (people stand around it).
- RESIDENTIAL: bench by door; firewood stack against wall; clay pots at entry;
  small side table; washing lines between posts; flower boxes; woodpile at side.
- FARM: cream grain-stack cones at barn; crates + sacks at barn doors; cart on
  lane (2 big wheels + bed); leaning tools; barrels at farmhouse; scarecrow in
  wheat; post+rail paddocks everywhere.
- HARBOR: cargo crates/barrels grouped on dock; rope coils; draped nets; mooring
  posts; beached rowboat; end-of-dock lantern; drying rack with fish rows.

## Answers props must give (who/what/why/relationship)
- Grain stack @ barn yard: farmers store harvest; beside barn doors; links
  field->barn. Bench @ house door facing path: residents sit, watch lane.
- Crates @ stall flank: vendors restock; beside counter, off the path edge.
- Rope coil + mooring post @ dock edge: fishers tie boats; at pile line.
- Signpost @ path forks: travelers choose; AT the fork, arms pointing.
- Lantern @ path intervals + dock end: walkers see at dusk; flanks, never mid-path.
- Bench AVOIDS: plaza center (gathering), door swings, path middle, field rows.
